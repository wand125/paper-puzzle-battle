How to play
  • The 61-cell board has 1,304,983 puzzle solutions, each with 25 black cells.
  • Take turns placing a black stone on an undetermined cell. Only solutions with that cell black remain.
  • The player whose stone leaves exactly one solution wins.
  • With redundant hints, an empty determined-black cell also accepts a stone and passes the turn. Determined-white cells are never playable.
